## Method and limits

IEEE Xplore document pages required JavaScript robot verification, preventing a complete direct author-page review. Records were checked against IEEE publisher-deposited metadata through the public Crossref API, indexed IEEE results, and selected official university records. New journal records contain the author’s exact ORCID and SUSTech affiliation. Conference records were matched to CV titles and coauthors. This is a verified update, not a claim of exhaustive Xplore coverage.

All additions were published by the audit date. Crossref record-creation dates were used only to check chronology, not as exact publication dates. Conference and journal versions remain distinct even when they share a title. No under-review work, private drafts, or same-name-only matches were added.

## Removing records

The website’s collapsed **Manage papers** section opens the corresponding file-deletion page on GitHub. The maintainer signs in, confirms and commits the deletion, and the GitHub Actions workflow rebuilds the site. A publication citation is removed by deleting its `publications/<id>.json`; an uploaded preprint is removed by deleting its PDF. No publication fallback array is retained. Public visitors cannot directly modify the repository.

Upload and removal controls activate after GitHub Pages setup. In the downloadable local preview, they are visibly disabled. The site does not embed GitHub credentials. Removal affects this homepage and its current deployed files, not publisher records; repository history retains previous versions.
