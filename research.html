Research interests

  • Information theory. Finite blocklength analysis, refined asymptotics, multiterminal source coding, and communication limits.
  • Statistical inference. Robust hypothesis testing, outlier detection, statistical classification, and sequence matching.
  • Learning and search. Statistical and active learning, noisy twenty questions estimation, and search for moving or multiple targets.
  • Wireless systems. Learning for communications, physical layer security, low-altitude wireless networks, and networked control.
