About

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Automation and Intelligent Manufacturing at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), Shenzhen, China. I received my B.E. degree in information engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2014 and my Ph.D. degree in electrical and computer engineering from the National University of Singapore in 2018. Previously, I was an Associate Professor in the School of Cyber Science and Technology at Beihang University and a Research Fellow in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the University of Michigan.

My research interests include information theory, statistical inference, physical-layer security, and wireless communications. I have coauthored two research monographs in Foundations and Trends in Communications and Information Theory, published by Now Publishers, and a book in the Springer Textbooks in Telecommunication Engineering series.

I am a Senior Member of IEEE and was recognized as a Young Scholar of the Chinese Information Theory Society in 2022. I have served on the technical program committees of IEEE ISIT, SSP, and ICCC, and currently serve as an Editor for IEEE Transactions on Communications.
