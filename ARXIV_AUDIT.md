# arXiv listing audit

Checked: 10 October 2026. The collection contains **48 verified public arXiv records**: **36 have published counterparts** and now appear as **[arXiv]** links after their Publications citations; **12 remain in Preprints**. One additional published survey was added to Publications during this status audit.

## Scope and method

Primary arXiv metadata supplied titles, ordered authors, submission/revision dates and URLs. Author identity was matched through explicit SUSTech/Beihang affiliations or exact works and established coauthors in the supplied CV and verified publication list. The current publication-status check used publisher-deposited Crossref metadata, publisher records, and official author/institutional publication lists. Individual JSON records retain URLs and the verification basis.

A related conference presentation does not by itself make a distinct extended arXiv manuscript published. Full journal/book counterparts are preferred where available. Title changes are matched only with supporting author/content/DOI evidence. No private drafts were copied into the website. Builds make no network requests and do not restore removed records.

The 12 records retained in Preprints have no confirmed full published counterpart in this bounded check; this is not proof that no counterpart exists. In particular, 2506.12308 is accepted for IEEE Communications Magazine according to official coauthor pages, but final publication has not been verified, so it remains in Preprints with a publication-pending note. The source titles and author orders are preserved.

## Special mappings and limits

- 1708.05496 maps to the full 2019 TIT article, whose title omits “Lossy,” rather than its two partial Globecom papers.
- 1808.05366 maps to the full Entropy article identified by its explicit related DOI, rather than the partial ISIT presentation.
- 2301.07871 maps to the 2023 Foundations and Trends monograph through its explicit DOI and journal reference.
- 2410.17540 maps to the 2026 TCOM article with “Spherical Codebooks” in its title. This is a strong inference from the identical ordered authors, distinctive title and matching abstract (which already describes spherical codebooks); arXiv does not explicitly cross-link the journal DOI.
- 2210.12736 remains a preprint. Its three conference papers are expressly described as partial presentations; no full published counterpart was verified.
- 1909.12954 and 2004.07231 are versions of the same noisy non-adaptive 20 questions work. Only the latter is retained; its source metadata preserves the earlier link.

## Editing and removal

Each arXiv record remains canonical in `arxiv/<id>.json`. Its `publicationStatus` and `publishedPublicationIds` determine placement. The homepage manager can remove the arXiv link independently of the publication citation. Deleting a publication does not relabel its published arXiv counterpart as an unpublished manuscript. Neither action changes the paper on arXiv. See README.md for upload folders and the separate AI-Aided Papers page.
